ABOUT / SCIENTIFIC CURIOSITYHUGO WEI

Between theory
and experiment.

My background spans applied mathematics, engineering, quantitative finance and computational research. My current independent research is focused on questions in fundamental and computational physics.

BACKGROUND

A quantitative
foundation.

I studied engineering at CentraleSupélec and management at ESCP Business School, alongside university-level applied mathematics. My earlier work includes numerical models of photonic quantum devices, financial market dynamics, and production-oriented quantitative modeling.

That combination shaped my approach: understanding an equation is inseparable from understanding its assumptions, how it is computed, and what an experiment would need to verify it.

Today I work on a set of public, reproducible research notebooks in quantum information, many-body physics, semiclassical gravity and scientific machine learning. These are ongoing projects rather than claims of peer-reviewed publication.
