BIG DATA / ENVIRONMENT / ETHICS

Big Data &
sustainability.

When does large-scale data help society, and when do its costs—in energy, privacy and power—outweigh the benefits?

THE RESEARCH / IN BRIEF

A technology with two sides.

This collaborative report, originally titled « Big Data : comment l'empêcher de devenir un cheval de Troie », examines the tension between the usefulness of large-scale data and the risks attached to collecting, storing and exploiting it.

It considers environmental impacts of digital infrastructure and data centres; benefits in healthcare, sports and business; risks to privacy and democratic life; and possible responses through regulation, anonymisation and better governance.

The report argues for more responsible oversight and explores whether data networks could be treated as a shared public resource. It is a historical student study: statistics and regulatory context reflect the period in which it was written, not necessarily current conditions.
