03 / WHAT CAN DATA TELL US?

Prediction is not causation.

Campaign teams can combine aggregate past election results and sociodemographic statistics to plan field outreach. Interviews yield a selected and potentially nonrepresentative sample. Text analysis summarizes responses, but neither activity proves that the resulting political strategy changed election outcomes.

The left expression represents an observational association; the right represents a causal intervention. Without an appropriate research design, moving from one to the other is unjustified. Here is a generic campaign intervention and an electoral outcome—not a trained vote-prediction model.
