BIG DATA / AI / POLITICAL ORGANIZATION · 2016

How data became a campaign's listening system.

In 2016, En Marche used statistical data to organize a national door-to-door listening effort, and semantic-analysis software to interpret thousands of responses. Explore the documented pipeline behind the Grande Marche.

Historical scope: the original collaborative ESCP paper discusses political Big Data and its ethical questions. This interactive case study adds external reporting on the Grande Marche. It explains how the tools were used, not whether they determined the 2017 election.

01 / THREE CONNECTED TECHNOLOGIES

Statistics. Fieldwork. Language AI.

The innovation was not a magic prediction engine. It was a workflow that linked publicly available geographical statistics, volunteers using mobile forms, and software analyzing large amounts of text.

GEOGRAPHICAL ANALYTICS

Liegey Muller Pons

LMP combined aggregate INSEE sociodemographic information with historical polling-station results to choose where the Grande Marche would conduct interviews. This describes planning, not individual voter prediction. Contemporary report ↗

MOBILE FIELD COLLECTION

Door-to-door interviews

Volunteers asked open-ended questions and recorded responses through an application. Reporting distinguishes roughly 25,000 completed questionnaires from the larger claim of 100,000 conversations. Methodology review ↗

SEMANTIC ANALYSIS

Proxem

Proxem analyzed reported survey text—about 1.5 million words—to organize themes, compare contextual associations, and produce a diagnostic. Its founder described a deep-learning-based approach; details of its model and accuracy were not independently verified here. Industry interview ↗

What the tools plausibly enabled

Geographical planning could coordinate volunteer fieldwork. Structured questionnaires and text processing could make many comments easier to review, search, and summarize. These are the documented operational roles of the systems.

What we cannot infer

These reports do not show that the campaign's analytics were necessary or sufficient for Macron's election, or establish a measurable change in votes caused by AI. The sampling method and representativeness of collected feedback also matter.
