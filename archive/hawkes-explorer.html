STOCHASTIC PROCESSES / AN INTERACTIVE INTRODUCTION

When an event silences another.

A Hawkes process models the timing of events whose occurrence changes the probability of future events. Positive interactions excite activity; negative interactions inhibit it.

This is an illustrative two-event-type model, not a fit to the empirical market data of the original 2024 CentraleSupélec team report.

From events to likelihood.
