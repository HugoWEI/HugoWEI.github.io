WHY A REVISED EDITION?

Better presentation.
More precise science.

The underlying 2024 abstract describes asset price SDEs, market beta and 2008-crisis validation, but the body is principally about event-count intensity estimation, with β representing exponential decay. The revised synopsis corrects that framing. The retained figure records parameter-estimation comparisons, not a verified forecast study.

This note is not a new peer-reviewed paper or a complete re-typeset replacement for the original team report. It is suitable as a public synopsis while the complete archival manuscript is being checked.
