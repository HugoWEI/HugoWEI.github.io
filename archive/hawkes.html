STOCHASTIC PROCESSES / FINANCIAL MARKETS

Hawkes processes
& inhibition.

Can a model of financial event arrivals account for interactions that suppress future activity as well as reinforce it?

THE RESEARCH / IN BRIEF

Modeling interactions between events.

In a classical Hawkes process, an event can increase the intensity of subsequent events. Our team studied multivariate Hawkes models with inhibitory interactions, allowing some events to reduce the conditional event intensity.

The report develops maximum-likelihood estimation with exponential kernels, uses numerical optimization and simulations, and examines applications to market-event data. A particular focus is the difficulty of identifying the decay parameters β and obtaining stable estimates.
