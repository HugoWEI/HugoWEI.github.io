# Publication audit — two historical group research reports

Review date: 30 October 2026. Scope: editorial and source-consistency checks of the two PDFs provided. **This is not yet a full line-by-line literature or computational audit.** The downloadable research synopses were written from the original reports and updated sources, not generated by rewriting every page of the historical documents.

## 1. CentraleSupélec — *Modélisation des phénomènes d’inhibition sur les marchés financiers* (2024, 25 pages)

**Preserved visual:** original figure 2 (box-plot parameter-estimation comparisons), kept with attribution and no invented numerical claims.

Items identified in the original:

1. **Abstract vs body mismatch.** The abstract speaks of stochastic price SDEs, price drops, 2008 crisis data, and market betas. The body largely treats signed multivariate Hawkes *event intensity* models, parameter estimation, empirical event series, and exponential kernel decays. A public version needs a complete accurate abstract.
2. **Decay-rate interpretation.** The report's `β_{ij}` determines the rate of decay of a Hawkes kernel; it is not the market-factor beta described in the abstract and introduction.
3. **Reference classification.** The Bonnet–Martinez Herrera–Sangnier reference is explicitly titled *...application to neuronal activity*. Its application domain must not be presented as a market-data study.
4. **Likelihood and positivity.** The log-likelihood must use intensity just before each arrival and a compensator integrating the rectified intensity. The expression and indexing of zero-crossing compensators require a mathematical proofread against the original cited formulation.
5. **Worked bivariate example.** The printed conditional-intensity equations appear to mix coefficients by event type. They should be recomputed directly from the parameter matrices and checked against the stated simplifying assumption `β_{ij}=β_i`.
6. **P-values and calibration.** The discussion claims inference from likelihood-ratio tests, but that section does not establish an actual sequence of tests and confidence intervals. Do not claim parameter significance without computing them.
7. **Complexity and convergence.** A measured `O(n)` trend over a finite range does not alone establish asymptotic algorithmic complexity. Box-plots or fixed-sample standard deviations do not prove estimator nonconvergence; distinguish numerical instability, weak identification and statistical consistency.
8. **Authorship.** All five original student coauthors and the supervising researcher must remain credited.

**Implemented in the public synopsis:** accurate event-intensity equation and log-likelihood; corrected decay-beta description; careful scope of numerical evidence; all five authors; original experimental figure.

**Required for a full replacement:** the original project LaTeX, BibTeX database and plotting scripts, permission from all coauthors for public redistribution, exact simulation seed/data and code checks, and an equation-by-equation pass.

## 2. ESCP — *Big Data : comment l’empêcher de devenir un cheval de Troie* (2023, 94 pages)

**Preserved visual:** original stakeholder map (PDF page 16) with project-level credit.

1. **Chronology.** 2019–2022 numbers and predictions for 2025/2030 must be marked as historical sources and projections; they must not appear as measurements current in 2026.
2. **Updated primary-source anchor.** IEA's *Energy and AI* (2025) reported around **415 TWh (2024 observed estimate)** for data-centre electricity use and **945 TWh (2030 base-case projection)**. The distinctions in years, scope and uncertainty are retained in the public synopsis. Source: https://www.iea.org/reports/energy-and-ai/executive-summary.
3. **EU law context.** The AI Act has staggered application dates; it is not a simple one-date transition or a replacement for the GDPR. Source: https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/policies/artificial-intelligence-act/.
4. **Third-party interviews.** Named and partially anonymized interview subjects are present in the appendix. Releasing interview text should be cleared against permissions, quote accuracy and privacy expectations. Some third-party statements require independent evidence if repeated as factual allegations.
5. **Figure rights.** The PDF contains many figures, logos and screenshots. Check whether sources permit redistribution of each figure in a derivative public document, not only citation in academic coursework.
6. **Attribution.** The 2023 report is coauthored by six students under Caroline Verzat. Preserve team attribution; do not market the group work as a sole-authored journal article.
7. **Genre.** This is a broad, interdisciplinary literature review and qualitative study, not a new mathematical theorem or empirical causal identification result.

**Implemented in the public synopsis:** updated framing, dated IEA statistic and projection, AI Act reference, the original team stakeholder diagram, author attribution, and omission of interview transcripts pending review.

**Required for a full replacement:** the editable Word/authoring document, all source images and bibliography, permissions for interview/figure reuse, and a systematic update of each factual numerical statement.
