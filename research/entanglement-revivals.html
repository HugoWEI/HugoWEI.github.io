RESEARCH / 02

When entanglement returns.

Phase-resolved entanglement and modular-capacity revivals in finite free-fermion chains.

◎
CURRENT STAGE: ANALYTIC–NUMERICAL INVESTIGATION
RESEARCH QUESTION

Can scattering phase and quasiparticle delay predict the location and shape of the first finite-size entanglement and modular-capacity revival?

Source repository ↗Other projects ↗Interactive derivation ↗
Finite quantum chains store information in quasiparticle propagation, boundary reflections, and coherent interference. A leading semiclassical picture captures ballistic arrivals; resolving the detailed revival structure requires careful phase information and finite-size corrections.
MATHEMATICAL FOCUS / SCHEMATIC
τW(k) = ∂δ(k)/∂ω(k)
01

Physical setup

The project investigates bipartite quantum information observables in finite free-fermion chains, building from open-chain Ising and related covariance-matrix computations. Boundary-return wavefronts and localized defects provide tunable scattering structure.

02

Method

Compare exact lattice or covariance evolution against coherent quasiparticle models, boundary return amplitudes, Wigner-delay corrections and Airy-type asymptotics. Analyze the first revival location as well as its height and width.

03

What is established

The repository contains numerical experiments and analytic research modules. Existing conformal and interface-defect results are used as calibration baselines; finite-size fits are not presented as new thermodynamic theorems.

04

What remains

Determine a regime with controlled errors in which the phase-corrected formula consistently reproduces benchmark dynamics, and isolate the part not already explained by published free-fermion asymptotics.
