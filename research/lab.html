01 / QUANTUM INFORMATION / EXACT ALGEBRA

The price of one more CNOT.

For a particular continuously parameterized two-qubit input state, compare the exact best achievable output Pauli fourth moments with at most three T gates and either at most one or two CNOTs. Vary the input parameter s.

PROOF + COEFFICIENTS ↗
PAULI FOURTH-MOMENT GAP0.01375751

Fourth-moment advantage G(s)

Derivation in a nutshell

Write R = ΣP⟨P⟩⁴ and M₂ = −log₂(R/4). The exact polynomial certificate compares the best restricted and unrestricted candidates over s ∈ [−1/8, 1/4].

N(s) = s² + 8s + 79
R₂(s) = P★(s) / [16 N(s)⁴]
R₁(s) = P₁(s) / N(s)⁴
G(s) = R₁(s) − R₂(s)
ΔM₂(s) = log₂[R₁(s) / R₂(s)]

What this demonstrates

The explicit one-parameter family needs two CNOTs to attain the circuit optimum under the repository's stated gate conventions. The finite polynomial and Bernstein positivity checks establish the model-specific claim, not a universal result for arbitrary two-qubit states.

Computational certificate: 3,134 exact polynomial-class dominance inequalities. The originality of this result relative to all prior publications has not been established.

How the peak is located

The Airy first-return predictor combines a clean propagation baseline with an asymptotic wraparound contribution. Its selected peak is compared with an independently computed two-site Hermite–Volterra witness.

t★ ∈ [tAiry − r, tAiry + r]

A precise scope

The enclosure is conditional on exact evaluations of numerical inputs, not a machine-certified interval result. The post-hoc diagonalization times, ≈26.42268 and ≈27.31195, fall within their respective reported conditional ranges.

It is a result for these two orientations and a specified smoothing rule, not a new general theorem about quantum revivals.

Why the crossing can be computed

Given f(r) = 1 − 2M/r − Λr²/3, solve for the black-hole and cosmological horizons. The geometric first laws make the integrated coarse-grained entropy proxy an algebraic expression in horizon areas.

f(rb,c) = 0,   Λ = 3 × 10⁻⁶, G = 1
Sb,c = π rb,c² / G
Sproxy(M) = Sb(M) + Sc(M) − Sb(M₀) − Sc(M₀)
Sproxy(Mcross) = Sb(Mcross)

Why this is not a Page curve

There is no computed fine-grained radiation entropy here. The project still needs a fully specified radiation subsystem and quantum state, global backreacted geometry, ultraviolet-controlled generalized entropy and competing QES saddle calculation.

The graph is a transparent, exact-equation explorer of a known thermodynamic benchmark, not a new physical prediction.

What the negative control shows

With the tested target and optimization setup, adjoint weighting makes global population RMSE worse for all four reported seeds. For the terminal-population error it is worse in three of four. This is more useful to display than to conceal.

Uniform population RMSE: 0.00306–0.00501
Adjoint population RMSE: 0.01154–0.04107
Model: zero-temperature independent qubit benchmark

The open research hypothesis

The adjoint targets the terminal amplitude, whereas the reported terminal observable is its square; the weighting objective is not automatically aligned with the evaluation metric. Next, the project requires a continuum-bath reference and a physically appropriate target-specific adjoint.
