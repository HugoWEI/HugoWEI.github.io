How does nonequilibrium semiclassical backreaction move quantum extremal surfaces and alter the transition between competing entropy saddles?

The generalized entropy of a candidate island depends on both geometry and the matter state. A backreacted metric and a coarse-grained entropy proxy cannot by themselves determine a fine-grained Page curve; the radiation algebra, matter entropy, and boundary conditions must also be specified.
MATHEMATICAL FOCUS / SCHEMATIC

01

Starting point

The repository reconstructs the classical Schwarzschild–de Sitter thermodynamic proxy used in a 2026 anomaly-backreaction study. It separates that proxy from the fine-grained radiation entropy to which a quantum extremal-surface calculation would apply.

02

Perturbative structure

For an isolated, nondegenerate QES of S_gen(q, ε) = S₀(q) + ε S₁(q) + ···, the first shift formally satisfies q₁ = −H₀⁻¹ ∇S₁(q₀). This is a universal implicit-function formula; computing a physical displacement requires the full SdS matter entropy and geometry.

03

Validated baseline

The thermodynamic-proxy identity, symbolic first-order sensitivity formulas and independent numerical checks have been reproduced in the repository. They do not amount to a calculated backreacted physical island or a new Page-time prediction.

04

Open problem

Define the radiation subsystem and quantum state, solve the time-dependent semiclassical equations in a fixed gauge, extremize both null directions and compare on-shell generalized entropies for all candidate saddles.
